Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This site, skova.dev, is the portfolio of Saeed Khoury. It is a static website. It has no sign-up, no login, no contact form, no comments, and no shop.

What personal information is collected

The site itself does not collect personal information. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and stores nothing in your browser.

You only share personal information if you choose to contact me by email, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn. That can include your name, your email address or phone number, and whatever you write in your message.

Technical data from the hosting provider

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. I do not receive this data as personal information about you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The pages load the Geist font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download it. See Google's privacy policy.

Why I use the information you send

  • To reply to your message.
  • To discuss a project, job, or collaboration you asked about.

I do not sell your information, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

How long I keep it

Messages stay in my email or WhatsApp history for as long as the conversation is useful. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your choices and how to contact me

You can ask to see, correct, or delete any personal information I hold about you. Email saed.khoury10@gmail.com or message me on WhatsApp. I will reply as soon as I can.

Other sites

This site links to other sites, including LinkedIn, GitHub, and client websites. They have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
